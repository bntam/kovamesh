Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · KovaMesh, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam · Contact: ngoctam.699@gmail.com

This policy explains what KovaMesh (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit bntam.github.io/kovamesh/ (future custom domain: kovamesh.dev), use the open-source kovamesh-verify CLI, or contact us — and what we do with it. It is short on purpose: we collect as little as we can run the service on.

1. Data we collect

  • Contact details you give us. If you email ngoctam.699@gmail.com or start a trial, we receive your email address and whatever you write in the message. We use it only to reply and, if you start a trial, to run your account.
  • Website usage data. Our hosting provider logs standard technical data for each visit: pages requested, date and time, browser and device type, and a truncated IP address used for security and abuse prevention. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.
  • Hosted-service data (Startup / Enterprise only). If you use the hosted loop, we process the repository metadata, PR diffs, and check results you send us in order to run verifications and return evidence bundles. We do not use your code to train models.
  • What we do not collect. The open-source kovamesh-verify CLI runs entirely on your machine or CI runner and sends nothing to KovaMesh. It only makes HTTP requests to the base URL you specify in your own spec file.

2. How we use data

  • To operate and secure the website and, where applicable, your hosted account.
  • To reply to enquiries and provide support.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which pages and docs are useful — never to identify individual visitors for advertising.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Cookies

This site uses no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If we add a strictly necessary cookie in future (for example, to remember a preference or keep a signed-in session working), we will note it here first. Your browser’s standard settings can block cookies; the site remains readable without them.

4. Sub-processors and hosting

The site is served as static files from a hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, or GitHub Pages — see our deployment notes). Those providers process the technical log data described in section 1 in order to serve the site. Trial and billing emails, if any, are handled through our email provider. We keep the list of processors to the minimum needed to run the service and will update this section if it changes.

5. Retention

  • Emails to us: kept as long as needed to handle your enquiry, then deleted on request or when no longer needed.
  • Hosted evidence bundles: Startup plans retain verdict history for 90 days; Enterprise retention is set in your agreement. Deleted accounts’ hosted data is removed within 30 days of closure.
  • Server logs: retained for up to 90 days for security, then deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email ngoctam.699@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request” and we will respond within 30 days. Where the law where you live gives you additional rights (including under Vietnam’s Personal Data Protection Decree 13/2023/ND-CP and, for visitors in the EU/UK, the GDPR), those rights apply in addition to this policy.

7. Security

We use HTTPS throughout, limit access to hosted data to the people who need it to run the service, and keep secrets out of repositories and logs. No system is perfect; if you find a security issue, please report it to ngoctam.699@gmail.com and we will acknowledge it promptly.

8. Children

KovaMesh is a developer tool for professional teams. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use data, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, note it on the site or by email to active trial users. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
